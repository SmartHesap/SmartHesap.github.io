Privacy Policy

Perfect Stop: Tap Timing · SMART HESAP
Last updated: November 1, 2026 · Türkçe

Summary

Perfect Stop has no accounts and we do not run any servers. Your progress stays on your device. The game shows optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch one. AdMob may collect device data to show and measure those ads, as described below. We never sell your data.

Data stored on your device

The game saves the following only on your device: best level and score, statistics, coins, power-ups, themes, daily reward streak, and your sound, vibration and language settings. This data is not sent to us and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads appear only when you tap a button to watch one (for example to continue a run or to double your coins). To deliver these ads, Google AdMob may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data.

Google uses this data to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, if you agree, personalize them. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the game asks for your consent (Google's consent form) before personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • iOS: the game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are not personalized using your IDFA.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the device settings.
  • If you decline personalization, you may still see non-personalized ads.

Leaderboards and achievements

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), your score, best level and achievements are sent there so they can appear on the world ranking under your Game Center or Play Games player name. Your best level places you in a league. This is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We do not receive or store this data.

Country flag: to show your flag next to your name, the game attaches your country code (taken from your device's region setting, for example "TR") to the scores it sends. This is an approximate location at country level only; no GPS or precise location is used. You can turn it off any time in Settings → "Show my flag"; new scores are then sent without a country.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the game creates an image card on your device and opens your system's share menu. The card goes only to the app and people you choose. We do not receive it.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
